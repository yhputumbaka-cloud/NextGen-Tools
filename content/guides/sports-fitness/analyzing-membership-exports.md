## What This Guide Covers
- How to export a fuller data file from your gym or studio platform for deeper analysis
- How to upload that file directly to Claude and get real analysis, not just a summary
- How to turn what you find into specific, actionable changes, not just interesting trivia

---

## Why This Matters
The weekly attendance habit is good for staying current, but won't surface slower moving patterns, like which membership tier is quietly driving most of your revenue.

---

## Case Study: Cinder Block CrossFit
Cinder Block CrossFit exported three months of membership and attendance data and uploaded it directly to Claude for analysis. One specific tier had meaningfully lower attendance and higher cancellation rates than the others.

**Result:** The owner used that finding to redesign the underperforming tier's onboarding process specifically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I do this deeper export and analysis?**
Quarterly is a reasonable cadence.

**Q: What file format works best for uploading to Claude?**
CSV or spreadsheet formats like XLSX both work well.

**Q: Is my data safe uploading it to Claude for this kind of analysis?**
Only upload what you need, and avoid member names if your platform allows excluding them.

**Q: What if the data reveals something uncomfortable, like an underperforming coach?**
Treat it as a starting point for a specific, constructive conversation, not a verdict.
