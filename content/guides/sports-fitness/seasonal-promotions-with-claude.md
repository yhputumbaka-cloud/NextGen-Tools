## What This Guide Covers
- How to plan promotions around the natural seasonal rhythms of a fitness business, especially January and pre-summer
- How to use Claude to generate a full small campaign, not just a single post, from one idea
- How to keep a simple running calendar so campaigns don't get planned at the last minute

---

## Why This Matters
Fitness businesses have some of the most predictable seasonal demand of any small business category. New Year's resolutions and pre-summer motivation are real, recurring waves of interest.

---

## Case Study: Bedrock CrossFit
Bedrock CrossFit wanted to run a January promotion but had historically just posted a single generic "New Year, New You" graphic. Using Claude, the owner turned a specific offer, a 6 week beginner onboarding program, into an Instagram caption, a Google Business post, an email, and a front desk sign.

**Result:** The gym's January sign ups increased over the prior year.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Map out your natural seasonal moments

```
I run a [type of fitness business]. Based on typical member 
behavior for this type of business, suggest 4 to 6 seasonal 
moments throughout the year that would make sense to build a 
promotion around, with a one line reason for each.
```

### Step 2: Pick one moment and describe the offer

### Step 3: Generate the full set of materials from one prompt

```
I'm running a promotion: [describe the offer, timeframe, and who 
it's for].

Generate a coordinated set of materials:
1. An Instagram caption, under 100 words
2. A Google Business post, under 1500 characters
3. A short promotional email with subject line
4. A one paragraph description for my website or booking page

Keep the tone consistent across all four, and avoid generic 
phrases like "New Year, New You."
```

### Step 4: Review for consistency and accuracy

### Step 5: Build a simple seasonal calendar for the year ahead

```
Based on the seasonal moments you suggested earlier, build a 
simple calendar showing roughly when I should start planning and 
promoting each one, working backward from the seasonal date.
```

### Step 6: Reuse and adjust each year

*Tip:* If you saved last year's materials, upload them into your Claude conversation and ask for an updated version rather than starting over.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How far in advance should I start promoting a January campaign?**
Early to mid December is common.

**Q: Should every campaign include a discount?**
No, a structured onboarding program or a limited time class series can be just as compelling.

**Q: What about slower months, not just the big seasonal spikes?**
Those are worth planning for too.

**Q: How many seasonal campaigns should I run per year?**
Four to six well planned campaigns tend to work better than trying to build something around every possible calendar moment.
