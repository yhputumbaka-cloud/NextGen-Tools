## What This Guide Covers
- How to build a private tracker for staff certification deadlines, CPR, AED, and coaching credentials, so nothing lapses unnoticed
- How to prompt Claude to build this as a persistent Claude Artifact
- How to pair it with calendar reminders so renewals happen with real lead time

**Requires Claude Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise.**

*This guide is a personal tracking tool, not legal or insurance advice. Confirm your specific certification and insurance requirements with the relevant certifying bodies and your insurer.*

---

## Why This Matters
A lapsed CPR or AED certification is a real safety gap for your facility, and depending on your setup, may affect your insurance or liability standing.

---

## Case Study: Apex Movement Collective
Apex Movement Collective, with a coaching team of six, built a certification tracker with Claude covering each coach's CPR, AED, and personal training certification expiration dates.

**Result:** No coach's certification lapsed during the following renewal cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this a substitute for knowing my actual insurance or certification requirements?**
No, confirm your specific requirements with your insurer and the relevant certifying bodies.

**Q: Should I ask staff to update their own records, or manage it centrally?**
Either can work, just make clear who's responsible.

**Q: What if a certification requires a course, not just a renewal fee?**
Set your calendar reminder further out.

**Q: Can this tracker also hold non certification staff information?**
It's built specifically around certification deadlines.
