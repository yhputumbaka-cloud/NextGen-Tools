## Why This Matters
By the time you've built a digital checklist, written SOPs, and maybe a sanitation reference, those tools tend to live in scattered links. A Notion hub brings them into one organized home base.

---

## Case Study: Amberlyn Salon Studio
Amberlyn Salon Studio had built a digital checklist and a set of written SOPs separately, each living as its own link. The owner used Claude to design a Notion workspace structure that organized both into one hub.

**Result:** New hires had a single place to start on day one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need the paid version of Notion for this?**
Notion offers a free plan with real functionality, though team size and some features vary by plan.

**Q: What if my team is small enough that this feels like overkill?**
Even a two or three person team benefits from having one clear place for procedures.

**Q: Should this replace the individual Artifact tools, like the checklist?**
No, it organizes and links to them rather than replacing them.

**Q: How often should I update the hub?**
Whenever an SOP changes or a new tool gets added.
