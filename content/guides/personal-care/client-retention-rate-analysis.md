## Why This Matters
A business with strong retention needs far fewer new clients to stay healthy. Some booking platforms calculate a retention or repeat client rate automatically, use it directly where available.

---

## Case Study: Hollow Creek Spa
Hollow Creek Spa assumed retention was strong based on familiar faces. The actual repeat client report told a different story, first time facial clients were rebooking at a lower rate than first time massage clients.

**Result:** The spa adjusted its first time facial follow up process specifically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's a good retention rate for a personal care business?**
This varies significantly, so there's no single universal benchmark.

**Q: Should I count someone as retained if they come back a year later?**
Define your own reasonable window based on how often clients would typically need your service.

**Q: Is this the same as the Cancellation Waitlist or Rebooking Messages guides?**
No, this is about measuring the bigger picture pattern over time.

**Q: What if retention is genuinely low and there's no obvious fix?**
Sometimes low retention reflects the nature of the service itself.
