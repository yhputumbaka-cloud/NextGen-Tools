---

## Why This Matters
Most booking and point of sale platforms already track which services and products are selling, but that data tends to sit in a reports tab nobody opens. The system already exists, the gap is turning it into something you understand and act on.

---

## Case Study: Marigold Wax Studio
Marigold Wax Studio had a reports tab that had never been opened past the first week of setup. The owner started pulling the weekly top services report and reading it with Claude every Monday morning.

**Result:** Within a month, the owner noticed a specific add on service was being requested far more than expected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if my platform doesn't have a reports section?**
Keeping a simple manual count for a few weeks is a reasonable starting point.

**Q: How far back should I look each week?**
The past 7 days is usually right for a weekly habit.

**Q: Is this worth doing if my business is very small?**
Yes, arguably more so.

**Q: Can Claude pull this data directly from my booking platform?**
Not without a specific integration set up for that platform.
