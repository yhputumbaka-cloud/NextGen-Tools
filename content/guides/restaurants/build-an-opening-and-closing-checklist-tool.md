## What This Guide Covers
- How to have Claude build an **interactive checklist tool with real checkboxes**, organized by role, directly in your Artifacts tab
- How to set it up so the checklist **resets each shift** rather than staying checked off from the one before
- How to keep it accessible on any phone or tablet your team uses during service

---

## Why This Matters
Opening and closing a restaurant involves dozens of small tasks. When these live only in someone's head, they get inconsistent fast. What actually works is something staff can pull up on their phone in thirty seconds, check off as they go, and hand off to a manager to confirm.

---

## Case Study: Pinewood Diner
Pinewood Diner had been relying on a single laminated checklist taped near the register for years, written for a kitchen layout that had changed twice since. Working with Claude, the owner rebuilt the checklist as an interactive tool in the Artifacts tab, organized by role, with checkboxes that reset between shifts.

**Result:** Closing shifts started finishing more consistently on time, and a manager who used to do a final walkthrough found far less to correct.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Walk Through a Real Shift and List Every Task
Walk through a real opening and closing shift and list every single task, including the ones that feel too obvious to write down.

### Step 2: Fill In Any Gaps with Claude

```
Here is my rough list of opening and closing tasks at [restaurant
name], organized by role: [paste your list]. Are there common
tasks for a [type and size] restaurant that might be missing?
Only suggest things that would genuinely apply to a restaurant
like ours, not a generic list.
```

### Step 3: Build the Tool Directly in the Artifacts Tab

```
Build me an interactive opening and closing checklist tool for my
restaurant. Here are the tasks organized by role and shift:
[paste your full verified task list].

For each task, include a checkbox staff can tap to mark complete.
Organize the tool with clear tabs or sections for Opening and
Closing, and within each, group tasks by role: [list your roles].
Include a manager sign-off section at the bottom of each shift
that confirms all sections are complete. Make it easy to reset all
checkboxes for the next shift with a single button.
```

### Step 4: Name It and Test It on a Phone
Give the artifact a clear name like "[Restaurant Name] Shift Checklists." Open it on an actual phone and check the reset button, sections, and sign-off area.

### Step 5: Put It Where Staff Will Actually Use It
Make sure every staff member knows the artifact exists and can find it, or have it open on a dedicated shared tablet.

### Step 6: Build It Into Onboarding
New hires get shown the checklist tool on their first shift, same as they'd be shown any other piece of equipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does the checklist actually reset between shifts automatically?**
Not automatically on a timer, a reset button clears all checkboxes manually.

**Q: What if two staff members are completing the same checklist simultaneously on different devices?**
Each device will show an independent version, a limitation worth being aware of.

**Q: How often should these checklists be updated?**
Whenever something changes meaningfully, and a general review every few months regardless.

**Q: Should every role have its own tab, or one shared checklist?**
Role-specific sections work better for most restaurants.

**Q: Can I add photos or diagrams to show how a task should be done?**
Not directly, but you can reference a separate SOP document for tasks where the how matters as much as the whether.
