## What This Guide Covers
- How to turn a quick spoken voice note into a **clean, usable piece of writing**, right after a service moment happens
- How to use this for **customer-facing messages, internal logs, and follow-up notes** without ever sitting down to type
- How to build a habit that fits the pace of a real shift, not one that requires quiet time you don't have

---

## Why This Matters
The best moment to capture a detail about a customer interaction is right after it happens, while it's still fresh. In practice, that's exactly when there's no time to sit down and type anything out. Claude can take a rough, spoken voice note and turn it into something clean and usable almost instantly.

---

## Case Study: Harbor Light Kitchen
A server at Harbor Light Kitchen kept meaning to log customer preferences and small complaints but never found time between tables to type anything coherent. After switching to quick voice notes dictated straight into Claude between courses, the restaurant started building a genuinely useful record of recurring customer details.

**Result:** Staff caught and resolved a recurring seating complaint within two weeks of starting the habit, something that had gone unaddressed for months.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Identify the Moments Worth a Voice Note
Focus on moments with real value: a customer's stated preference or allergy, a complaint worth following up on, a compliment worth passing to the kitchen, or a detail about a regular.

### Step 2: Dictate the Note Directly
Use your phone's voice input to speak directly into a Claude conversation, exactly as it comes to you, rough phrasing and all.

### Step 3: Ask Claude to Clean It Up

```
Clean this up into a short, clear log entry I can save. Keep the
key facts accurate, remove filler words, and format it so someone
else on staff could read it and understand exactly what happened
and what to do about it.
```

### Step 4: Route It to the Right Place

```
Based on this note, should this go into a customer record, get
flagged to a manager, or passed along to the kitchen? Draft a short
version appropriate for wherever it should go.
```

### Step 5: Turn a Note Into a Follow-Up Message When Needed

```
Based on this note, draft a short follow-up message to the customer,
in our brand voice, addressing what they raised.
```

### Step 6: Build the Habit Into Your Shift Rhythm
Pick a few natural pause points during a shift to run through any notes worth capturing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a special app for the voice input?**
No. Most phones have built-in voice-to-text you can use directly when typing into Claude.

**Q: What if my voice note is messy or I trail off mid-thought?**
That's completely fine, cleaning it up is exactly the point of Step 3.

**Q: Should every staff member do this, or just managers?**
It works best when anyone who interacts directly with customers can use it.

**Q: What if a note contains something sensitive, like a complaint about a specific employee?**
Route that directly to a manager as a private conversation rather than a shared log.

**Q: Can this replace a proper POS note field?**
If your POS has a notes field, this can feed into it, clean up the voice note with Claude, then copy the result in.
