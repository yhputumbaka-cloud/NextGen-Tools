- How to set up a **simple, automated newsletter sequence** that doesn't require daily attention once it's running
- How to use Claude for both the **strategy and the copy**, not just the writing
- How to build a **content calendar** so you're never staring at a blank email the morning it's due

---

## Why This Matters
An email list is one of the few marketing channels a restaurant fully owns. The problem most owners run into isn't building the list, it's keeping up with it. **A predictable, even modest rhythm performs far better than an impressive one-off that never repeats.**

This guide isn't about generating more email copy. It's about building a system, a welcome sequence that runs automatically and a monthly rhythm that doesn't depend on remembering at the last minute.

---

## Case Study: Birchwood Tavern
Birchwood Tavern had collected over 600 email addresses through a sign-up sheet at the host stand, but had sent exactly two newsletters in the previous year. Working with Claude, the owner built a three-email welcome sequence and a repeatable monthly template, then loaded both into MailerLite's free tier.

**Result:** The automated welcome sequence alone generated more reservations in its first month than the two manual newsletters had produced over the entire previous year combined.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Set Up Your Email Platform
Sign up for a free email platform like MailerLite or Mailchimp. Import your existing list of customer emails, however you've been collecting them.

### Step 2: Build Your Welcome Sequence with Claude

```
Help me write a three-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to
[restaurant name]'s email list. Our brand voice is [paste your voice
guide or describe briefly].

Email 1, sent immediately: a warm welcome and one thing that makes
us distinct.
Email 2, sent three days later: introduce a signature dish or our
story.
Email 3, sent one week later: a small incentive to visit, like
[describe an offer, e.g. a free appetizer with their first visit].
Keep each email under 150 words.
```

### Step 3: Load the Sequence Into Your Platform
In MailerLite or Mailchimp, find the Automation section and set up a workflow triggered by "subscriber joins list." Test it yourself before it goes live to real subscribers.

### Step 4: Plan a Repeatable Monthly Template

```
Help me design a simple, repeatable monthly newsletter template for
[restaurant name]. It should include: one seasonal or featured dish,
one upcoming event or date worth mentioning, and one small personal
note from me as the owner. Keep the total length under 200 words.
```

### Step 5: Build a Content Calendar a Few Months Out

```
Help me sketch a content calendar for the next three monthly
newsletters. For each month, suggest a seasonal angle, a dish or
event to feature, and a one-line theme, based on [list any known
upcoming events, holidays, or seasonal menu changes].
```

### Step 6: Write Each Month's Email Using the Template

```
Using our newsletter template and this month's theme, [paste the
relevant line from your content calendar], write this month's full
newsletter. Include a clear subject line and preview text too.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I actually send a newsletter?**
Once a month is a sustainable, effective baseline for most independent restaurants.

**Q: What if I don't have 600 email addresses like the case study?**
The welcome sequence works the same regardless of list size, and a smaller list is a good time to set this habit up properly.

**Q: Free email platforms, is there a catch?**
The free tiers of MailerLite and Mailchimp do have subscriber and send limits, but those limits are typically far beyond what one independent restaurant location needs.

**Q: Can I use the welcome sequence for something other than a list sign-up, like a loyalty program?**
Yes, the same three-email structure works for any new-subscriber moment.

**Q: What's a realistic open rate I should expect?**
Restaurant newsletters often see open rates well above general marketing email averages, since the list is made up of people who already chose to visit you.
